## Nov 8, evening: more coaching built in (Damon: "there is not enough AI tooling built in")

The student still goes first. After they try, the coach can show and suggest, on the student's press, and everything it writes sits in a dashed box labeled as the coach's. New module `coach.py` (plugin only; `ld_method.py` stays the web app's mirror, untouched); routes under `coach/` in `dashboard/plugin_api.py`; page in `desktop/src/coach.js`.

1. **Coach chat on every floor.** **Coach** in the rail opens a drawer beside the floor (a bottom sheet under 900 px). The backend reads what is on screen from the student's own saved work by id: the question, the take's transcript, the prep outline, the extemp file (numbered [S1]..), the ballot, the case and the round, the figure, line and critique. One thread per floor. On the Case Workbench the button goes to the existing coach panel instead. `POST coach/chat`.
2. **Extemp.** After a 60-second answer's ballot: **Show me a strong answer** (about 140 words, built on the student's attempt, three notes on why it works, what it kept). After the openings coach: **Show me a strong opening**, built on the coach's pick. In prep, once Your answer is written: **Suggest areas** (three, each with one source from the student's file; Put it in Area n, Pick this source). **Find me evidence**: picks from the file only, each marked supports, against or background; when the model fails, the file is searched in code and the page says so. `coach/extemp/example`, `coach/extemp/areas`, `coach/extemp/evidence`.
3. **Lincoln-Douglas Case Workbench.** Under each section: **Suggest contentions**, **Suggest a value and criterion**, **Write a block to an attack** (paste the attack), and in More, **Strengthen this card** (the selected card, which must be in the case). Drafts are kept under the section (`case.suggestions`) and in the coach thread; **Add to <section>** puts one into the case prefixed "[Coach's suggestion: rewrite it in your own words]". Evidence it cannot know stays `[find a card: ...]`. `coach/ld/suggest`.
4. **Rhetoric, Write.** After the critique: **Show me three versions** of the student's own line with the figure. The backend refuses until that line was critiqued (`coach.json`). `coach/rhetoric/versions`.
5. **Ballots.** **Show me** under every ballot's drill (Ballots view, the LD ballot, the full-round extemp ballot; not on the 60-second floor, which has Show me a strong answer): the weakest moment in the student's exact words (checked, flagged if not found), why it cost them, a modeled redo, how to practice. Kept with the ballot. For an LD ballot whose round is gone, only the judge's quoted lines are used, and the coach is told so. `coach/ballot/show`.
6. **Settings, Coach examples** (on by default). Off: every example route answers 400 with "Coach examples are off in Settings...", no example button renders, persisted LD suggestions are hidden, and the chat runs under a no-examples stance (explain, ask, critique; never writes a sentence to say).

**The check, in code (`coach.check`)**, on every example, note, draft, redo and chat answer before the page sees it: a [S#] that is not in the file becomes "Not a source in your file. Do not cite it."; a valid one becomes "(Outlet, Mon D, YYYY)" in the coach's own example text (strong answer, opening, redo, areas, evidence), or just the date when the sentence already names the outlet. Chat and feedback that discuss the student's sourcing (example notes, what was kept, a redo's why and practice), and any sentence about the student's own citing ("You named Reuters [S1] but not the date"), never get a date: the token is dropped when the outlet is named, or becomes "(Outlet)" (fix after review, `v0.2-cite-fix`); any quoted run of 4+ words not in a source or the student's own words (transcript, question, case, line), any figure of two or more digits or any percent not found there (times and lengths like "20 seconds" pass), any month-and-day date that is not a source's date, and any of 38 well-known outlet names not in the file mark the line. The box's foot says what was checked and how many marks to look at. Prompts: specific to each task, an 8th-grade register, age-appropriate, fair on contested questions (clear answer, the other side's strongest point, no parties or people praised or attacked), and every model call through `Model.json`/`Model.text` with the existing guards. Model errors and answers that miss the shape reach the page as one plain sentence ("The coach's answer could not be read. Ask again.").

**Tests (43 new, `tests/test_coach.py`):** the switch on by default, set only to a boolean, and every example route refusing with no model call when off; the chat's off stance; attempt first on every route (no words, no answer line, no critique, an empty case, a card not in the case, a too-short attack, wrong-floor takes); every kind of mark (bad [S#], outlet, quote, figure, date) and the marks a model writes itself stripped; areas with a source number not in the file; evidence picks deduplicated and dropped; the evidence fallback; non-schema answers and a broken model; Show me kept and not re-asked, a misquoted moment flagged, an LD ballot from the judge's lines; the chat's context per floor and a page-supplied prompt ignored; the bundle calling every route and gating every example button on the switch; the docs no longer saying the coach never writes.
